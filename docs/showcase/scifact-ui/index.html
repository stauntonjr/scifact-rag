Live application recording · September 18, 2026

From scientific claim to inspectable evidence.

This recording follows one request through the actual SciFact RAG interface running on a single NVIDIA DGX Spark: enter a claim, generate a qualified answer, and open the cited parent-document evidence.

What happens

  1. The recorded claim is entered into the live evidence inspector.
  2. The selected retrieval pipeline ranks evidence from the public SciFact corpus.
  3. Local Qwen generation returns a qualified answer with parent-document citations.
  4. A citation moves directly to the complete evidence supplied to generation.

Recording boundary

Claim: Side effects of antidepressants increase the risk of stroke.
